# The Storefront

The shop your members see. There are two pages, and they are both public.

# The catalog: `/store`

![The store catalog](/user-manual/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/storefront.png)

- Every **published** product as a tile: image, title, short description and its cheapest price ("From
  €3.00 / month").
- **Category filters** across the top. Only categories holding at least one published product appear.
  Picking one shows that category's description above the products.
- A **search box** that matches titles and short descriptions.
- A **My purchases** button, for logged-in members.

Draft and archived products are not listed, and their pages return a 404.

The category filter and the search both live in the URL, so `/store?category=merch` and `/store?q=hoodie`
are links you can share, put in a menu, or send to a member.

# A product page: `/store/your-product-slug`

- The **image gallery**: cover image first, then the gallery, with thumbnails, arrow-key navigation and a
  full-size view.
- Your **description**.
- The **tier comparison**: every tier side by side, with its feature list, its billing options, and a buy
  button. The tier you marked **Recommended** is highlighted.

With only one tier, the buy box moves into the page header instead of a comparison — a single product at a
single price is one click from the top of the page.

The page works without JavaScript: the first image and the first price are rendered server-side and the buy
button submits normally. JavaScript adds the gallery and makes the big price follow the billing option the
customer selects.

# Getting members there

Nothing links to the store until you add it. **Admin Panel → Menu Builder** → add an item of type **Route**
→ pick `ecommerce_store`.

Other things worth linking:

- A single product: a **URL** item pointing at `/store/your-product-slug`.
- A category: `/store?category=your-category-slug`.
- **My purchases**: the route `ecommerce_orders`, useful in a member or profile menu if you sell
  subscriptions.
- Your own landing page built with the [CMS widgets](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/cms-widgets).

Because menu items carry permissions, you can restrict who sees the link — a members-only shop is a menu
item limited to a role.

<Alert severity="info">
    Menu permissions hide the link, not the pages. <code>/store</code> stays reachable to anyone who has the
    URL, and checkout always requires a login. There is no "hidden product" setting: a product is either
    published and visible to everyone, or it is Draft and cannot be bought at all.
</Alert>

# Link previews and search engines

Store pages come with their own titles, descriptions, canonical URLs and social preview tags, so a product
link pasted into Discord, X, Facebook or Slack unfurls into a proper card with your cover image.

Product pages also publish structured data describing the product and its prices, which is what lets search
engines show price information in results. None of this needs configuring — it comes from the title, the
short description and the cover image you already filled in.

<Alert severity="info">
    The social preview is a 1200×630 crop of your <strong>cover image</strong>. If your product links look
    wrong when shared, that image is the thing to fix.
</Alert>

# Theming

The storefront is styled entirely with your theme's own colours, fonts and radii — it inherits whatever your
community looks like rather than imposing a look of its own. Switching theme restyles the store with it.

Every section of the store templates is a named, overridable block, so a custom theme can replace any part
of a product page, a tier card or a plan card without forking the whole template. That is developer
territory; see the [customization guides](/guides/customization/introduction).

# Where customers manage their purchases

`/store/orders` — **My purchases**, linked from the top of the catalog. Their subscriptions as cards and
their full payment history below. It is where they download invoices, cancel a subscription and fix a failed
payment.
