# Troubleshooting

The things that actually go wrong, and what to do about them.

# Orders stay on "Pending"

**By far the most common problem, and it is almost always the webhook.**

A pending order means the store created it and never heard back that it was paid. Check, in this order:

1. **Is the webhook endpoint configured?** In your Stripe dashboard under **Developers → Webhooks**, there
   should be an endpoint with the URL shown on the Stripe page in your admin panel.
2. **Is it in the right mode?** A test-mode endpoint does not receive live events, and vice versa. If you
   switched to live keys, you need a live-mode endpoint too.
3. **Is the signing secret right?** Each endpoint has its own `whsec_…` secret. Pasting the test one while
   running live keys means every webhook is rejected as unsigned.
4. **Are the right events selected?** The endpoint needs all **checkout**, **invoice**, **customer** and
   **charge** events. `checkout.session.completed` is the one that settles a purchase; `invoice.paid` is
   the one that renews a subscription.
5. **Can Stripe reach your site?** It must be publicly reachable over HTTPS with a valid certificate.
   Stripe's webhook page shows the response it got for each attempt — a 404, a timeout or a certificate
   error tells you what to fix.

<Alert severity="info">
    Stripe retries failed webhooks for days. Once you fix the endpoint, pending orders usually settle
    themselves as the retries land. You can also hit <strong>Resend</strong> on a specific event in the
    Stripe dashboard.
</Alert>

If the payment genuinely went through and you would rather not wait, opening the customer's order page
re-checks with the provider — so asking them to open their order from **My purchases** often fixes it on the
spot.

# A customer paid but has not received anything

Look at the order status.

- **Paid** — the money is in and it is waiting for fulfillment. If the product is delivered by hand, it is
  in your queue under **Orders → Paid**. If it is supposed to be instant, something got stuck: hand over
  the goods and use **Mark as completed**.
- **Completed** — the store thinks it is done. If roles were involved, check the member's roles in
  **Admin Panel → Users**; if it was shipped, check the fulfillment details on the order.
- **Pending** — see above.

# A customer did not get their role or badge

- Roles are granted when the payment arrives. If the order is still **Pending**, nothing has been granted
  yet.
- Check the product actually lists the role under **Grant roles** — and that it is not a system role, which
  cannot be granted.
- If they had the role **before** buying, the store leaves it alone at the end too. That is intentional.
- After a subscription ends, a role is only removed if no other live purchase grants it and they did not
  already have it.

# A subscription that should have ended is still active

Past-due subscriptions are ended by a scheduled task. If it never runs, they never end.

Check that your site's **scheduler and message consumer are running** — see
[Background workers](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/store-settings#background-workers). On a self-hosted install
this is the single most common cause of subscription weirdness.

# "This product is not available right now"

The customer clicked a buy button whose price no longer exists or is no longer active. Usually one of:

- the tier's price was **deactivated** and no active one is left;
- the product was **archived** or set back to **Draft**;
- a [Buy Button widget](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/cms-widgets#buy-button) points at a tier whose prices are
  all inactive.

Re-activate a price, or add a new one to that tier.

# I cannot edit a price

Because it has been sold. A sold price is locked forever so that existing orders and invoices keep saying
what actually happened.

Use **Change price** to replace it with a new one; existing subscribers stay on the old amount. See
[Locked prices](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/tiers-and-prices#locked-prices).

# I cannot delete a product

Because it has been sold. **Archive** it instead: it disappears from the store, keeps all its history, and
existing subscriptions keep running until you cancel them.

# I cannot delete a price or a tier

- A **sold** price cannot be deleted. Untick **Active** to stop offering it.
- A tier's **last** price cannot be deleted, because every tier needs one. Delete the tier instead, or
  deactivate the price.
- A tier with **sold** prices cannot be deleted at all.

# Prices show the wrong currency

Prices keep the currency they were created with. If you changed the store currency after creating prices,
the old ones did not move.

Create new prices at the correct currency (using **Change price** on sold ones) and deactivate the old ones.

# The store does not appear anywhere on my site

It is not linked automatically. Add it in **Admin Panel → Menu Builder** as a **Route** item pointing at
`ecommerce_store`. See [The Storefront](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/storefront#getting-members-there).

# A product is missing from the store

- Its status must be **Published**. Draft and Archived products are not listed and their pages 404.
- It needs at least one tier with an **active** price.
- If you are looking at a filtered catalog, check it is in that category.

# Emails are not arriving

See [Notifications → If nothing arrives](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/notifications#if-nothing-arrives).

# A refund did not revoke anything

- **Partial refunds** are deliberately ignored by the store: the order keeps its status and nothing is
  revoked. Only a full refund counts.
- A refund revokes **that order's** grants only, and never something the member already had or that another
  live purchase also grants.
- A subscription's roles and badges last as long as the subscription. Refunding one of its payments from the
  admin panel ends it; refunding in Stripe's dashboard does not, so the subscription — and its access — keeps
  running until you cancel it from **Ecommerce → Subscriptions** or in Stripe.

# Something in Stripe has the wrong name

Stripe product names are set once, the first time a price is sold. Renaming a product or tier in your store
does not rename it in Stripe, so old customers' invoices keep the old name.

If the name matters, fix it directly in your Stripe dashboard.

# Getting help

When you report a problem, these make it answerable straight away:

- the **order reference** (the code on the order page);
- the order's **status** and its **provider reference**;
- whether your provider is in **test or live** mode;
- what the **webhook attempts** look like in your provider's dashboard.
