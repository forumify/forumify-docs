# Notifications

The store sends its own notifications, so you do not have to wire up confirmation emails yourself. Every
one of them is both an **in-app notification** and an **email**, following each member's own notification
settings.

Two deliberate silences:

- **A successful renewal sends nothing.** Their payment provider already emails a receipt, and a monthly
  "thanks for paying again" is a monthly invitation to cancel.
- **An abandoned checkout sends nothing.** Someone who browsed away from the payment page has not failed at
  anything, and should not be told the next day that their payment failed.

<Alert severity="info">
    An instantly delivered purchase results in exactly <strong>one</strong> email: "your order is complete".
    An order you fulfill by hand results in two — one when the money lands, one when you hand it over.
</Alert>

# To your team

<table>
    <thead>
        <tr>
            <th>Who gets it</th>
            <th>What</th>
            <th>When</th>
        </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
        <tr>
            <td>Everyone with the product's <strong>Fulfilled by</strong> role</td>
            <td><strong>New order to fulfill</strong></td>
            <td>A manually fulfilled order is paid, including each renewal</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
            <td>Everyone with the product's <strong>Fulfilled by</strong> role</td>
            <td><strong>Cancellation to handle</strong></td>
            <td>A manually fulfilled subscription is cancelled or ends, and once more if its paid period runs out with the request still open</td>
        </tr>
    </tbody>
</table>

Both come from the **Fulfilled by** role set on the product, so who gets told is your choice per product: a
shipping crew for merch, a game admin for server perks.

<Alert severity="warning">
    <strong>Refund requests have no notification.</strong> Any product can have a refund window, so there is
    no obvious role to send them to. The count next to the <strong>Refunds</strong> menu item is what tells
    you one is waiting.
</Alert>

# Where they go

- **In-app**, in the member's notification list, linking straight to the order or subscription.
- **By email**, using your site's normal mail setup and templates. Order emails include the fulfillment
  details — a shipping address, the username someone entered — so your team can act straight from their
  inbox.

Members control what reaches them per channel in their own notification settings, like every other forumify
notification.

# If nothing arrives

Store notifications are sent through your site's background workers. If your emails are not arriving:

1. Check that the **message consumer is running** — see
   [Background workers](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/store-settings#background-workers).
2. Check your site's **mail configuration**, by triggering any other forumify email.
3. Check that the notification was created at all: if it is missing from the in-app list too, the event that
   triggers it never happened — which usually means a
   [webhook problem](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/troubleshooting).

A mail failure never breaks a payment. Orders are recorded and roles granted whether or not the email goes
out.
