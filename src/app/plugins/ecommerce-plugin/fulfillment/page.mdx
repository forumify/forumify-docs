# Fulfillment

Fulfillment is how a customer actually gets what they bought, after the money has arrived.

**Paid is not completed.** *Paid* means the payment went through. *Completed* means the customer has the
thing. For an instant purchase those are the same moment; for anything you hand over yourself, the order
waits in between — and that waiting list is your to-do list.

# Choosing a fulfillment type

Every product has exactly one fulfillment type, set on the product form.

Changing the type only affects **new** orders. Orders already placed keep the type they were bought under,
so a product you switch from shipped to instant does not suddenly lose the addresses you still have to ship
to.

<Alert severity="info">
    One type per product. If two variants of a thing are delivered differently, they are two products, not
    two tiers of one.
</Alert>

## Delivered instantly

The default. The order goes straight from paid to completed, and the customer gets their confirmation
immediately.

This is the right choice whenever the purchase does not require a human: a supporter subscription that
[grants a role](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/roles-and-badges), a donation, an access pass.

## Delivered by an admin

For things you hand over yourself. Two extra settings appear:

- **Ask the customer for** — an optional question asked at checkout, such as "Minecraft username" or
  "Discord tag". Whatever the customer types is shown on the order, in your notification, and on their own
  order page. Leave it empty if you do not need anything from them.
- **Fulfilled by** — the role responsible. **Everyone with this role is notified** when one of these orders
  is paid. It is required for manual types.

## Shipped

For physical goods. At checkout the customer fills in a shipping address:

Full name, address, address line 2, postal code, city, state or region, and country.

![The shipping address at checkout](/user-manual/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/checkout-shipping.png)

The address is shown on the order for you to ship to, and on the customer's own order page so they can check
what they entered. A returning customer's address is prefilled from their last shipped order.

**Fulfilled by** is required here too — that role is who gets told there is a parcel to send.

<Alert severity="info">
    Shipping costs, rates and stock levels are not part of the plugin. Your price includes shipping, so if
    you ship internationally, either build that into the price or sell separate tiers per region.
</Alert>

# Working through paid orders

**Admin Panel → Ecommerce → Orders**, and set the status filter to **Paid**. That is your fulfillment queue:
everything that has been paid for and not yet handed over.

The **Orders** menu item shows the count in brackets — **Orders (3)** — so you can see at a glance whether
anything is waiting.

## Marking an order as completed

Open the order and use **Mark as completed**.

![Complete an order](/user-manual/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/order-complete.png)

- **Note for the customer** — optional. A tracking link, a code, an explanation. It is shown on their order
  page and included in the notification they receive.
- The order moves to **Completed**, records who completed it, and the customer gets their "order is
  complete" email.

If the product is refundable, note that the refund clock starts when you complete the order, not when it was
paid. A customer waiting on you does not lose refund days.

## Completing an order that should have been automatic

The action is available for any paid order, including instant ones. You would use it when an integration got
stuck: the customer has their thing, but the order never moved off **Paid**. The form asks you to confirm
that you understand it is normally automatic, so nobody does it by accident.

# Subscriptions and fulfillment

A subscription renewal of a **manually fulfilled** product stays on **Paid** every period, so a monthly
merch box or a monthly handover lands in your queue again each time. Renewals of every other type complete
by themselves — the subscription simply continues.

## Cancellations that need you

When a subscription of a **manually fulfilled** product is cancelled or ends, the store opens a
**cancellation request**: something was handed over by hand, so someone has to take it back by hand.

- Everyone with the product's **Fulfilled by** role is notified.
- The **Subscriptions** menu item carries the count of open ones.
- The subscription page has a **Mark as handled** action once you have closed it out.
- The customer's plan card says "Cancellation requested. An admin is finishing it." until you do.
- If the paid period runs out and the request is still open, the store reminds you once.

Products that are delivered instantly never produce these — there is nothing to tear down.

# What customers see

On their order page:

- **Pending** — "We are waiting for your payment to be confirmed." The page refreshes itself.
- **Paid** — "Payment received. Your order is being processed." Plus whatever they entered at checkout, so
  they can check their shipping address.
- **Completed** — "Thank you! Your order is complete.", with your note if you left one.

They are emailed at both steps: once when payment arrives (only for orders that are not instant), and once
when you complete it.

<Alert severity="info">
    The gap between paid and completed is the part customers worry about. A one-line note when you complete
    the order — a tracking number, "sent to your Discord DMs" — does more for support load than anything
    else in this chapter.
</Alert>
