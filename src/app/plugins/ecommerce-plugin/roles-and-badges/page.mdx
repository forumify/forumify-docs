# Roles &amp; Badges

A product can hand out forumify **roles** and **badges** when someone buys it, and take them back when their
subscription ends or their order is refunded. This is how you sell access: supporter ranks, private forums,
donator perks, premium sections.

# Setting it up

On the product form:

- **Grant roles** — the roles a buyer receives once they have paid.
- **Grant badges** — the badges a buyer receives once they have paid.

![Granted roles and badges](/user-manual/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/product-grants.png)

Both accept several at once. System roles cannot be granted.

<Alert severity="info">
    Granting is independent of the fulfillment type and <strong>stacks with it</strong>. A hoodie can be
    shipped by hand <em>and</em> grant a "Supporter" badge. A product does not have to choose.
</Alert>

# When they are granted

The moment the **payment arrives** — not when you complete the order. Someone who buys a shipped product
that also grants a role gets the role immediately, and waits for the parcel separately.

A granted badge also sends the platform's usual "you earned a badge" notification, so it shows up the same
way badges awarded any other way do.

Free products grant too: a free tier still produces an order, and that order still grants.

# When they are taken back

            <td>Never revoked, unless it is refunded</td>
        </tr>
    </tbody>
</table>

That last row matters: a one-time purchase is permanent by nature. If you want access that can be taken
away, sell it as a subscription.

## Two rules that prevent accidents

The store is careful about taking things away, because a role is rarely only about the shop.

**1. Roles someone already had are never taken away.** If a member already had the "Supporter" role when
they bought a product that grants it, ending their subscription leaves it alone. The store only removes
what it gave.

**2. Overlapping purchases do not cancel each other out.** If a member has two subscriptions that both grant
"VIP" and one ends, they keep "VIP" until the last one is gone.

<Alert severity="info">
    Each grant is recorded individually — who got what, from which order, and whether they already had it.
    Removing a role manually in Admin Panel → Users will not confuse the store, but it also will not update
    the store's record: if they still have an active subscription, the role is not re-granted automatically.
</Alert>

# A worked example: a supporter subscription

1. Create the role and the badge you want to hand out, under **Admin Panel → Roles** and **Badges**.
2. Give the role access to whatever it should unlock — a private forum, a CMS page, a higher upload limit.
3. Create a product called "Supporter", fulfillment type **Delivered instantly**.
4. Under **Grant roles** pick "Supporter". Under **Grant badges** pick your supporter badge.
5. Add a tier "Supporter" with a recurring price of €5 per month, and maybe a yearly one at €50.
6. Publish it.

From then on: someone subscribes, gets the role and badge within seconds, keeps them as long as they keep
paying, and loses them when the subscription ends — or when their payment keeps failing past the grace
period.

# Things to watch

- **Deleting a role** that a product grants: the product simply stops granting it. Existing members keep
  whatever they have.
- **Changing what a product grants** only affects new purchases. Members who bought under the old setup keep
  what they were given, and lose exactly that when their subscription ends.
- **Do not grant an admin role from a product.** Anyone with a card could then buy their way into your admin
  panel.
