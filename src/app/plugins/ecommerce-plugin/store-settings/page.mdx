# Store Settings

Everything that applies to the store as a whole: what you charge in, who can manage it, and where your
members find it.

# Configuration

**Admin Panel → Ecommerce → Configuration**

![Ecommerce configuration](/user-manual/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/configuration.png)

- **Store currency**: all prices in your store use this currency.

That is the whole page, on purpose. A store has one currency, and it is stamped onto every price you create.

## About the currency

Available currencies: AUD, BRL, CAD, CHF, CNY, CZK, DKK, EUR, GBP, HKD, INR, JPY, KRW, MXN, NOK, NZD, PLN,
SEK, SGD, USD and ZAR.

<Alert severity="warning">
    Changing the currency later does <strong>not</strong> convert anything. Existing prices keep the
    currency they were created with, so you would end up with a store selling in two currencies at once.
    Decide on it before you create prices.
</Alert>

Your payment provider has to support the currency you pick. If it does not, checkout fails at the provider
and the order is marked as failed.

Some useful combinations:

- **A shipping helper**: `admin.view` + `orders.view` + `orders.fulfill`. They see what needs sending and
  mark it done, and cannot touch prices or keys.
- **A support moderator**: add `orders.refund` and `subscriptions.view` + `subscriptions.manage` so they can
  answer billing questions and cancel a subscription.
- **A shop editor**: `products.*` and `categories.*`, with no access to orders or payment providers.

<Alert severity="info">
    <strong>Payment provider credentials are sensitive.</strong> Anyone with
    <code>payment_providers.manage</code> can replace your Stripe keys, and thereby redirect where your money
    goes. Keep that permission on the roles you would trust with the Stripe dashboard itself.
</Alert>

# Where customers find the store

Nothing links to the store until you add it yourself. That is deliberate: your menu is where you decide what
members see, and the plugin does not force an entry into it.

**Admin Panel → Menu Builder**, add a menu item:

- **Type**: Route
- **Route**: `ecommerce_store`
- **Label**: whatever fits your community — "Store", "Shop", "Support us"

You can also link to a single product (`/store/your-product-slug`), or to a CMS page you built yourself with
the [Ecommerce widgets](/plugins/ecommerce-plugin/cms-widgets). Because the menu carries permissions, you can
also limit who sees the entry — a members-only shop is just a menu item restricted to a role.

<Alert severity="info">
    Menu permissions hide the <em>link</em>, not the pages. The storefront itself is public, so anyone with
    the URL can reach it. Checkout always requires a login.
</Alert>

## Customers find their own purchases at `/store/orders`

Logged-in members get a **My purchases** button at the top of the store catalog, where they see their
subscriptions and their whole payment history. It is worth adding to your member menu as well if you sell subscriptions — it is the page
where they cancel, download invoices and fix a failed payment.

# Background workers

Two parts of the store run in the background and need your site's workers to be running:

- **The message consumer** sends notification emails. Without it, nothing in the store fails, but your
  customers never receive their confirmations.
- **The scheduler** ends subscriptions whose grace period has run out and sends reminders about
  cancellations that still need handling.

If your site is hosted on forumify cloud this is already taken care of. On a self-hosted install, see the
[self-hosting guide](/guides/self-hosting).
